# The crop gate

Branch `crop-gate`, commit a844774 (frozen before any Tayynsha 2025 result existed).

## Why

On the Tayynsha 2026 run, 10 of 49 bare fallow regions got a cut. All ten steps fall on 1-14 August, mostly on
small regions: bare soil drying in the August heat brightens in B11 like a cut. Bare land counts as ripe from
1 August (NDVI already under 0.4), so the rule looks for a rise on it at once.

Diagnosis used `data/tayynsha/tools/rule.py`, a Python copy of the harvest command's dating that matches the Java
output on all 2,496 regions of the ten squares.

## Rule

A field whose NDVI never reached 0.5 on a usable reading from May to August gets `no crop seen`, not a cut. A crop
canopy reaches 0.6-0.85; on the 2026 district, 2,466 of 2,496 field regions peaked at 0.5 or more.

## First results (not the held-out test)

| | bare fallow with a cut | field regions turned "no crop seen" |
|---|---|---|
| Tayynsha 2026, 10 squares (data the gate was set on) | 10 of 49 -> 1 of 49 | 14 of 2,496 |
| 8 old seasons (not used to set it) | 21 of 67 -> 3 of 67 (Kostanay 2023: 15 -> 2) | 3 |
| hand-read cuts, Kokshetau 2025-2026 | 14 of 22 within one photo, unchanged; 0 false fires, unchanged | |

The bare fallow check overlaps the gate's own definition (both look at land that never greened), so it flatters the
gate; the stronger evidence is that it touches almost no real field. Held-out test: the Tayynsha 2025 run, with and
without the gate.
