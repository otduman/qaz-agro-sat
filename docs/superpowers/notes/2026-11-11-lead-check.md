# Are the leads real? First checks (2026-11-11)

After merging the crop gate (main 909208a), all 24 district square-seasons were re-dated and rejoined.
District, core squares: 2026 2,533 fields (321,487 ha), dated 52% of area, overlap 84 of 94; 2025 2,158 fields
(225,321 ha), dated 63%, overlap 81 of 83. Holders read for three squares (about 75,500 ha, 76-79% of field area).

## Lateness (halfway date against fields within 20 km) does not hold up yet

- Year to year: across 31 holders counted in both seasons, correlation of days behind 2025 vs 2026 is 0.04; same
  sign in 12 of 25. Being late in one season says nothing about the next.
- Against chance (2026, 13 companies with 3+ dated fields): median distance of a company's halfway date from the
  area's is 7 days, the same as random groups of fields of the same size (7.0); beyond 10 days 31% vs 29%.
- But a company's own fields are cut closer together than random fields (sd 9.3 vs 12.8 days): the grouping is real,
  the halfway date is just not where it shows.

## Duration (10% to 90% of a company's dated area) is the plan's actual lead, and looks more promising

2026, 7 companies with 6+ dated fields, against 500 random groups of the same size: the largest company
(about 10,000 ha dated) took 46 days against 30 for random groups (as long or longer in 7% of draws); the others
are within chance (24-80%). One hint, not a finding: 7 tests, one season.

## What is missing

Crop type (peas and barley are cut before wheat, oilseeds after; a farm's crop mix moves its dates), more holders
(three squares), a fair duration test on 2025, and any ground truth.
