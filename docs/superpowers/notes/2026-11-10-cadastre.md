# Linking fields to farms: what the public cadastre offers

10 November 2026. Step 7 of the Kokshetau run. Reading published sources only; no account, no owner data downloaded.
A direct look at the map's data service was stopped (automated access to a government site is not ours to assume).

## What the sources say

- **There is a national public cadastral map** (публичная кадастровая карта), part of the unified real-estate
  cadastre (ЕГКН), at https://map.gov4c.kz/egkn/. It combines the land, legal and town-planning cadastres. Rules for
  it were approved in July 2023 and replaced by a new order on 13 August 2026 (No. 483/НҚ), which I have not read.
- **Per plot it shows** cadastral number, area, purpose and type of right (2023 press coverage).
- **Names of land users were opened by law from 1 January 2022**: "names of individuals and company names of all land
  users", in the land cadastre's map (then aisgzk.kz). The Ministry of Agriculture said in February 2022 that the
  resource holds "full information about the subjects who received a land plot, with all grounds".
- **But** in 2023 the digital ministry said part of the owner data is not displayed, under the personal-data law.
  Which part (individuals only, or also peasant farms and companies) is not stated.
- **Downloads** of search results (PDF, XLSX, DOCX) are for logged-in users (eGov login), per the 2023 rules.
- **Related public registers:** JerKarta (plots returned to the state, with outline, area and user) and the Ministry
  of Agriculture's list of users whose land was taken back by court.

## What is not known

- Whether today's map shows the land user's name for farm plots near Kokshetau without a login, and for which kinds of
  user (company, peasant farm, individual).
- The terms for reusing the data commercially, and for automated access. Nothing found either way.

## What it means

Linking fields to farms looks possible in principle: the law made land users public, and plots carry cadastral numbers
and outlines. It is not confirmed in practice. The cheapest check is for the owner to open the map in a browser, click
a few large fields north-west of Kokshetau (around 68.85 E, 53.90 N), and note what the card shows. If names of
companies show, a demo could link fields by hand for one district; anything at scale needs the terms of use, and legal
advice before storing names of individuals.
